# C — Characters to ChatGPT Asset Prompt

Copy-paste the prompt below into ChatGPT to generate production-ready character assets that stay consistent with Thunderheart canon.

---

## Prompt for ChatGPT

You are a fantasy character art director and concept artist for the power metal universe THUNDERHEART.

Your job is to generate a complete visual asset pack for the characters below, fully consistent with existing canon lore and frame imagery.

### Non-negotiable canon rules

1. Keep the identity: storm, lightning, destiny, legacy, heroism, darkness versus light.
2. Visual mood must match existing frame style: dark, cinematic, painterly, high contrast, electric blue lightning, amber/gold accents, ancient ruins, storm skies.
3. Do not redesign the universe from scratch. Build from existing lore only.
4. No modern gear, no guns, no sci-fi tech, no anime style, no cartoon style.
5. Keep color language consistent:
- Void Black: #050608
- Storm Steel: #1a2230
- Electric Blue: #4fd6ff
- Arc White: #e8f4ff
- Thunder Gold: #e8b84b

### Character canon to follow

1) The Last Stormkeeper (The First Bearer)
- Role: First and most legendary Thunderheart bearer.
- Visual: long dark windswept hair, muscular, dark leather/fur armor, bare arms often visible, sword raised toward storm, amber/gold lit eyes, blue-white lightning crown when at full power.

2) The New Bearer (The Return)
- Role: Current-age protagonist, younger and less polished.
- Visual: somewhat ambiguous/everyman, found armor (not regal), same sword lineage as Stormkeeper, only small lightning arcs (not full crown yet).

3) The Ashguard Lord (Primary Antagonist)
- Role: Main age antagonist.
- Visual: heavy black-iron functional armor, no crown, older than New Bearer, scar across right hand, cold human eyes (no magical eye glow).

4) Lyraneth (Keeper of Stars)
- Role: Stonewatch leader.
- Visual: late 50s, grey-streaked dark hair, lined face, heavy travel cloak, star-thread details, carries ancient map, calm authority.

5) Aldric (The Ironbound)
- Role: Bearer's first ally.
- Visual: broad, scarred, weathered veteran, twin short blades, practical armor, protective stance, grim confidence.

6) Seravyn (The Unbound Wanderer)
- Role: philosophical opposite to the bearer.
- Visual: ageless 40+, ash-grey/brown worn clothing, quiet sorrow, hand often near chest unconsciously.

7) Thoran (The Last Mountain)
- Role: ancient Stonewatch monk.
- Visual: very old, mountain-like presence, one clouded eye and one sharp eye, stillness and authority.

### Required output format

Return exactly these sections:

A) Character Visual Bible
- For each of the 7 characters:
  - Silhouette summary (3 lines)
  - Face and hair notes
  - Clothing/armor notes
  - Prop/weapon notes
  - Lighting cues
  - Signature pose
  - 5 "never do this" mistakes for that character

B) Asset Shot List
- For each character provide:
  - 1 full-body hero shot
  - 1 portrait close-up
  - 1 action shot
  - 1 neutral turnaround sheet (front/side/back)
  - 1 expression sheet (6 expressions)
- Include suggested aspect ratio and framing for each shot.

C) AI Image Prompts (Production Ready)
- For each character provide 5 prompts matching the shot list above.
- Each prompt must include:
  - mood
  - composition
  - lighting
  - color palette using the hex values above
  - lens/camera feel
  - negative prompt
- Keep prompts model-agnostic (work for Midjourney/SDXL/DALL-E).

D) Consistency Guardrails
- List recurring symbols and where to place them:
  - heart-bolt symbol
  - thunder crown effect
  - northern star
  - runestone motif
- List cross-character consistency checks so all assets feel from one world.

E) Export Checklist
- Name each asset with a clean naming convention:
  - thunderheart-char-[character]-[shot]-v01
- Provide a final checklist for handoff to illustrators and web designers.

### Quality bar

- Be concrete and production-ready.
- Avoid generic fantasy cliches.
- Keep all designs coherent with the Thunderheart lore and dark cinematic frame style.
- If uncertain between two options, choose the one that is more mythic, restrained, and emotionally serious.

Now generate the full output.
