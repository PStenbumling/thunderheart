## Frame Analysis — The Canon Visual Sequence

The five frames are not static artworks — they are a **cinematic sequence**. Together they show the transformation from darkness to full storm power. This is the central visual metaphor for the entire band: dormant → awakening → rising → full power → identity.

| Frame | State | Lightning | Dominant color | Narrative moment |
|-------|-------|-----------|---------------|-----------------|
| frame-1.png | Before — The Call | None (dark clouds only) | Near-black, charcoal, amber glint | The sword is raised but the storm hasn't responded yet. Anticipation. |
| frame-2.png | Awakening | Single blue bolt — sword tip only | Deep blue begins | First contact. The Heart has recognized the bearer. |
| frame-3.png | Rising | Full bolt + branching sky | Cobalt blue dominant | The storm is responding. The bearer is committed. |
| frame-4.png | Full Storm | Explosive branching — fills the sky | Electric blue-white, debris | The Thunder Crown is manifesting. Maximum power. |
| frame-5.png | Identity | Total lightning saturation | Pure blue-white + brand gold | The THUNDERHEART logo. This is who we are. |

**The website currently uses frame-1.png as the static background.** Cycling through all five frames as the user scrolls or as time passes is one of the most impactful improvements possible (see G — Website Integration).

## Persistent Visual Elements Across All Five Frames

These elements appear in every frame and are therefore **canonical visual constants**:

1. **The warrior figure** — long dark hair, dark leather/fur armor, sword raised overhead
2. **The high vantage point** — the warrior always stands on rock or ruins above everything else
3. **The dark castle / Fallen Crown** — visible in background, left of center, warm amber windows (the only warmth in cold scenes)
4. **The runestone / grave marker** — lower right corner, ancient stone with carved markings
5. **The silhouette sword** — straight blade, raised at a ~80° angle toward the sky
6. **The lightning/thunder relationship** — lightning always originates AT the sword tip and RADIATES outward, never falls from sky alone
7. **The oil-painting style** — heavy detail, visible texture, dramatic chiaroscuro (dark figures with brilliant light sources)

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Void Black** | `#050608` | Background base, deep shadows — the absence of storm |
| **Storm Steel** | `#1a2230` | Secondary dark, armor, stone textures |
| **Electric Blue** | `#4fd6ff` | Lightning, primary energy — the Heart's power signature |
| **Arc White** | `#e8f4ff` | Lightning core, maximum intensity moments |
| **Thunder Gold** | `#e8b84b` | The Heart flame, Thunder Crown border, the Fallen Crown's windows, brand metallic |

### Secondary Colors

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Deep Cobalt** | `#2a7ea3` | Mid-tone blue, storm depth, UI secondary elements |
| **Charcoal Cloud** | `#2d3446` | Storm clouds, rock surfaces |
| **Frost Star** | `#b8d4f0` | Northern Star glow, cold ambient light |
| **Ember Amber** | `#7a4a12` | Distant fire, forge glow, warm counterpoint |
| **Blood Iron** | `#3d1a1a` | The Ashguard's color — deep red-black, not heroic |

### Color Rules

1. **Electric Blue is the brand color.** When in doubt, lean blue. Not cyan, not purple — the exact `#4fd6ff` from the site variables.
2. **Thunder Gold never competes with Electric Blue** — one dominates per scene. Blue = battle power. Gold = the flame's warmth and the legacy's weight.
3. **The Ashguard scenes use zero blue.** Their world is Blood Iron and Charcoal — no lightning, no star, no warmth.
4. **White only appears at the absolute center of a lightning bolt.** Nowhere else. White is reserved for maximum intensity.
5. **The Northern Star** is always Frost Star (`#b8d4f0`) — not Electric Blue. The Star is colder, quieter, more distant than the Heart's power.

## Composition Rules

### Frame Composition (Portrait ratio — all five frames are tall/narrow)

1. **Vertical thirds:** The warrior occupies the middle third. The sky (storm, lightning) occupies the top third. The rocks and ruins occupy the bottom third.
2. **The warrior is never centered perfectly.** Slightly left of center, creating a diagonal of energy from lower-left (rock base) to upper-right (sword tip and lightning origin).
3. **The horizon line, when visible, is always below center.** The sky dominates. The storm is the world.
4. **The Fallen Crown castle appears upper-left — always.** It anchors the left edge and provides depth.
5. **The runestone is lower-right — always.** It anchors the past, grounds the mythology.
6. **No foreground clutter.** The warrior's silhouette must be clean against the sky.

### Landscape/Wide Compositions (for website scenes and album art)

1. **The storm always comes from behind the heroic figure** — pushing them forward, not pushing them back.
2. **Enemy/dark forces are always lower in the frame.** The bearer is always elevated.
3. **Use the diagonal rule for energy flow:** from lower-left dark → upper-right light. This is the visual grammar of the entire brand.
4. **Leave negative space for text overlays.** Bottom quarter and top quarter should be compositable.

### Character Portraits

1. **Eye contact is earned.** The Last Stormkeeper never makes eye contact — he looks toward the storm. The New Bearer occasionally looks at the viewer — they are uncertain, reaching for connection.
2. **Lighting always comes from above and slightly in front** — the lightning origin. Never from below (that reads as villainous/horror).
3. **The Thunder Crown appears as a suggestion, not a crown shape** — fractured arcs of electricity at the top of the frame, not a neat ring.

## Lighting Style

**Style reference:** Chiaroscuro meets lightning photography meets oil painting.  
More specifically: **dramatic theatrical lighting from a single powerful overhead source** (the storm/lightning), with warm amber fill light from the lower-right (the Fallen Crown's windows, implied campfire, forge).

### Lighting Rules

1. **Primary light source: the lightning bolt.** Cool blue-white. Hard light. Creates strong shadows.
2. **Secondary light source: ambient fire/warmth.** Amber-gold. Soft light. Fills the deepest shadows on the warrior's near side.
3. **No neutral white light.** Everything is either cool (storm) or warm (flame). The clash of cool/warm IS the visual tension of the saga.
4. **Shadows fall toward the camera** — the viewer is in the darkness; the hero faces the storm.
5. **Bloom / glow** on lightning — the electric blue should bleed at its edges into the surrounding clouds. This is the Heart's energy leaking into the world.
6. **Never flat.** Every surface should have at least two light sources creating modeled depth.

## Recurring Symbols — Visual Grammar

### The Heart-Bolt (Primary Brand Symbol)
- Heart shape, bisected vertically by a lightning bolt
- As seen on frame-5.png with "THUNDERHEART" logotype
- Colors: deep grey heart body, electric blue bolt, thunder gold border/outline
- Always legible at small sizes — never ornate beyond necessity

### The Raised Sword
- Straight blade, no ornament, tip pointing toward lightning source
- The sword is the lightning rod — it conducts the Heart's power, not stores it
- Visual rule: when the sword appears alone (no warrior), it should look ancient and worn, not heroic-pristine

### The Thunder Crown
- Never drawn as a neat crown
- Always: fractured, branching arcs of blue-white electricity above a figure's head
- Should look painful and magnificent simultaneously — not comfortable to wear

### The Northern Star
- Single point light in a dark field
- Surrounded by a subtle frost-blue corona — ice, not fire
- Should appear small — it is far away. Its power is persistence, not size.
- Rule: if the star appears in an image, it should be the only light source that has no visible origin. Everything else has a cause. The Star simply is.

### The Runestone
- Appears in all five frames (lower-right)
- Ancient carved stone, weathered, with the Heart-Bolt symbol just barely visible
- Size relative to the warrior: about knee-height
- Meaning: the past is always present. Every new bearer finds these stones.

## Style Consistency Rules for External Artists

1. **Reference frame-3.png or frame-4.png for the "full power" baseline.**
2. **Do not add color outside the approved palette.** No greens, no purples, no oranges.
3. **Do not make the warrior look clean.** Weathered, scarred, armored for function not appearance.
4. **Do not add ornate metalwork or fantasy decoration** — the aesthetic is brutal-heroic, not high fantasy elvish.
5. **The lightning bolt in the logo must always be a straight fork** — not curved, not stylized beyond the existing brand mark.
6. **Text treatments:** All caps, wide letter-spacing, condensed font weight. The existing site uses the approach correctly.
