# F — AI Art Prompts

> 15 production-ready prompts organized by category.  
> Every prompt is grounded in the canon frames and Story Bible.  
> All prompts follow the format: mood · composition · lighting · color palette · camera/lens feel · negative prompt.

---

## How to Use These Prompts

- These are written for **Midjourney v6+**, **Stable Diffusion XL**, or **DALL-E 3**
- Adjust `--ar` (aspect ratio) per category: portraits = `--ar 2:3`, landscapes = `--ar 16:9`, frames = `--ar 1:3`
- Core style anchor to prepend to all prompts: `epic fantasy oil painting, dramatic chiaroscuro, ultra-detailed, cinematic, dark heroic fantasy`
- Core negative prompt to append to all prompts (base): `modern clothing, guns, technology, anime, cartoon, flat lighting, ugly, deformed, low quality, blurry, oversaturated, neon colors, purple, green`
