# B — Story Bible

> World rules, timeline, factions, locations, and the symbol system.  
> This is the foundation every song, visual, and lyric must be consistent with.

---

## 1. World Rules

These are absolute. No story element should contradict them.

1. **The Heart is not a weapon — it is a covenant.** It grants no invincibility. It grants the refusal to surrender.
2. **The Heart cannot be taken by force.** Only voluntarily passed at the moment of a bearer's death, or given to a willing successor. Stolen power corrupts.
3. **Only one bearer exists at a time.** While the Heart sleeps, no Stormkeeper walks the earth.
4. **The bearer is not chosen by worthiness — only by willingness.** A great warrior who refuses cannot receive it. A frightened common person who stands anyway can.
5. **The Northern Star appears twice:** once when a bearer is near death (a warning / last call), and once when a new bearer awakens (a signal to the world).
6. **The Thunder Crown is not a physical object.** It is a manifestation of the Heart's power — it appears around the bearer's head as crackling lightning during moments of full power. It cannot be removed, melted, or stolen.
7. **The storm always responds to the bearer.** Weather is not commanded — it is a reflection of the bearer's inner state. Calm warriors walk in silence. Raging warriors call lightning.
8. **Darkness is not a single entity.** It is a state of the world when courage has abandoned it. It can be embodied by tyrants, monsters, or apathy — but it is never truly killed. It retreats.
9. **The flame mentioned in "The Last Stormkeeper" is the inner fire of the Heart.** Every bearer tends this flame. If it dies, the Heart sleeps again. It does not extinguish through battle — only through despair.
10. **Every age is named.** Ages are defined by who carries the Heart, not by calendars.

## 2. The Five Ages — Timeline

### AGE I — BEFORE (The Primordial Storm)
*"Long before kingdoms were carved from stone..."*

The world is young and violent. Titans — immense beings of elemental force — war across the sky. This is the **Great Thunder War**. Its duration is measured in burning skies, not years. No human civilization survives intact. Most history from this era is myth, even within the story universe.

**Key event:** The final Titan strikes the last warrior who refuses to kneel. The bolt transforms him. The Titans are spent. The war ends.

**Mood:** Apocalyptic, primordial, awe-inspiring. Darkness and fire. No color — only the white-hot edge of creation tearing itself apart.

---

### AGE II — RISE (The Age of the Last Stormkeeper)
*"One man stood against the night..."*

The First Bearer — who becomes known as the **Last Stormkeeper** in legend — walks the broken world. He does not conquer it. He guards it. He forges no empire. He holds the edge between hope and total collapse. His age is long. His enemies are legion.

This age ends with his fall — not in defeat, but in completion. His body can no longer carry the storm. The Heart sleeps.

**Song:** *The Last Stormkeeper*  
**Mood:** Heroic, ancient, mythic. Like a legend being born in real time.

---

### AGE III — FALL (The Long Silence)
*"His power could not die... only sleep."*

The world without a Stormkeeper. Darkness spreads. Kingdoms rise and collapse. Tyrants rule. Monsters walk unchecked. The name Thunderheart becomes myth, then legend, then a children's story dismissed by scholars. The stones that mark the old battles are worn smooth.

But the Northern Star still burns. Always in the same place in the sky. Those who notice it keep the old faith.

**No song yet for this age.** — *Opportunity for future album.*  
**Mood:** Cold, slow, creeping despair. Grey skies. Faded glory.

---

### AGE IV — RETURN (The New Bearer)
*"When that sound returns... the darkness knows its time has ended."*

The world is on its knees again. The Northern Star blazes unlike any night in memory. Somewhere, an ordinary person feels thunder begin in their chest — uninvited, impossible to ignore. They do not understand what is happening. They only know they cannot walk away.

This age covers the new bearer learning what they carry, finding allies, and taking the first stand. The storm recognizes them. The world does not yet.

**Songs:** *Northern Star*, *Ride the Storm*  
**Mood:** Discovery, fear, determination. The weight of something ancient pressing into something modern.

---

### AGE V — LEGACY (The Eternal Chain)
*"ThunderHeart is not a man. It is a legacy."*

The current age. Every battle since the Return is part of this age. The bearer is no longer searching — they are fighting. The line of Stormkeepers stretches back into myth and forward into whatever comes next.

This age is ongoing. The saga is not finished.

**Song:** *Stand and Fight*  
**Mood:** Defiant, timeless, thunderous. This is the battle cry of every bearer across every age.

## 3. Factions

### The Stormbound
Followers, warriors, and companions who have sworn their lives to the Thunderheart bearer of their age. They do not worship the bearer — they fight alongside them. Bound by oath, not blood. Their sigil is a **lightning bolt over an open hand.**

### The Ashguard
The standing armies of tyrants and dark powers. They are not a single nation — they are whatever force embodies the age's darkest tendency. In each age they wear different armor, carry different names. Their common trait: they fight to silence hope.

### The Stonewatch
Ancient keepers of the old stories. Not warriors. Scholars, monks, wanderers. They maintain the runestones that mark old Stormkeeper battle sites, preserve the songs (including this one), and watch for the Northern Star. They are the ones who recognize a new bearer before anyone else does.

### The Unbound
Those who heard the storm's call and refused it. Some turned bitter and resentful — dangerous because they know the lore. Some simply walked away — neither enemy nor ally. A quiet tragic thread in the universe.

## 4. Key Locations

### The Highrock
The cliff edge where the original Titan bolt struck. Sacred ground. Always stormy. A runestone stands here — ancient, worn, unmarked except for a thunderbolt scar burned into it at the moment of the First Bearer's transformation. Appearing in frame imagery: the stone marker in the bottom right of the frames.

### The Thunder Reach
The mountain range where the last great battle of each age tends to occur. Ancient and jagged, permanently storm-wracked. The sky here never fully clears.

### The Fallen Crown
A ruined fortress (the dark castle visible in the background of all five frames). It was once the seat of the greatest Ashguard tyrant. Now abandoned, haunted, used as a waypoint by the Stonewatch. Lightning strikes it regularly. The bearer sometimes dreams about it.

### The Starpath
Not a physical place — a route visible only when the Northern Star burns brightest. Following the Starpath leads a new bearer to the Highrock and to the dormant Heart. It is a journey that takes as long as it must.

### The Last Threshold
A concept-location more than a physical one: the moment before total defeat, the edge of the last stand. Every Stormkeeper has faced it. None have crossed it into surrender.

## 5. The Symbol System

### The Heart (⚡♡)
The central symbol — a stylized heart bisected by a lightning bolt. **This is the band logo.** In the story universe it appears burned into stone at every Stormkeeper battle site. New bearers sometimes find it carved where they least expect it.

- Color: **deep crimson** interior, **electric blue** bolt, **hammered gold** border
- Meaning: life + storm + legacy

### The Crown (⚡👑)
Not a physical crown. A halo of lightning that manifests above the bearer's head in moments of full power. In artwork it should appear as branching blue-white electricity — never a neat crown shape. Wild, dangerous, beautiful.

- Color: **ice white** core, **cobalt blue** branches
- Meaning: authority not granted — earned and carried

### The Star (✦)
A single point of cold blue-white light in an otherwise dark sky. Always at the same celestial position. Navigators use it. Believers watch it. When it burns brighter than normal, the Stonewatch sends runners.

- Color: **cold white** with **pale ice blue** corona
- Meaning: guidance, hope that cannot be extinguished, the soul of every fallen bearer

### The Flame (🔥)
The inner fire of the Thunderheart. It appears in lyrics as the "guardian of the flame" — meaning the bearer's most important duty is to keep the Heart's fire alive. Represented visually as a small amber flame inside the heart symbol.

- Color: **amber gold** (#e8b84b — matches existing site gold variable)
- Meaning: courage, the will to continue, the opposite of despair

### The Storm (⛈)
Not a symbol so much as a state. When storm imagery appears — gathering clouds, still wind before thunder, distant rumble — in lyrics or artwork, it signals a Thunderheart event. The storm is never random in this universe.

- Visual rule: storms move toward the bearer, not away
- Lightning always strikes upward from the bearer's sword, never down from sky alone
