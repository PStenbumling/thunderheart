    The Legend

    Long before kingdoms were carved from stone... before the first sword was forged... before men learned to fear the darkness... there was only the Storm.

    When the heavens were torn apart by the Great Thunder War, the sky burned for a thousand nights. Titans clashed. Mountains shattered. Oceans rose. The world stood on the edge of oblivion.

    Then came the final strike.

    Not of fire.

    Not of light.

    But of destiny.

    It pierced the heart of the last warrior who refused to kneel.

    Instead of death, the storm became one with him.

    His heart transformed into an eternal source of thunder, courage, and unbreakable will.

    From that moment...

    He became THUNDERHEART.

    He was never a king.

    Never a god.

    Never a conqueror.

    He was the guardian between hope and darkness.

    A lone warrior destined to rise whenever the world stood on the brink of ruin.

    When tyrants sought dominion...

    When monsters walked the earth...

    When fear silenced the brave...

    The storm answered.

    But ThunderHeart was never meant to live forever.

    His power could not die...

    Only sleep.

    When one bearer falls, the Heart chooses another.

    Not the strongest.

    Not the richest.

    Not the chosen by birth.

    Only those willing to stand alone against impossible odds.

    For ThunderHeart is not a man.

    It is a legacy.

    They say the signs always come first.

    The wind grows still.

    Clouds gather beneath a clear sky.

    The distant rumble echoes across forgotten mountains.

    And somewhere...

    A heartbeat begins to thunder.

    When that sound returns...

    The darkness knows its time has ended.
